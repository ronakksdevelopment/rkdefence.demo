Built around exam day, not the classroom

For over a decade, RK Defence Academy has taken students from their first physical training session to their final selection parade, with faculty who have trained recruits for the Army, CRPF and Tripura Police.

Structured programs

Every batch follows a fixed syllabus map across reasoning, mathematics, general knowledge and Hindi, tracked week by week.

Experienced trainers

Faculty who have served or trained recruits for the Army, Air Force, Navy, CRPF and Tripura Police lead every class.

Physical + written training

Morning drills and running build toward exam-day timings; classroom hours build toward exam-day scores.

Mission-oriented vision

We track every trainee from enrolment to result, because a coaching centre succeeds only when its students are selected.

How a trainee moves through the academy

Four stages, run in order, from the first week to the selection parade.

1

Physical conditioning

Daily PT, running and drills, building toward each force's timing standard.

2

Academic coaching

Subject-wise classes in reasoning, maths, GK/GS and Hindi with a written syllabus tracker.

3

Mock tests & assessment

Timed mock papers modelled on the real exam pattern, reviewed score by score.

4

Interview & documentation

Personality development and document guidance ahead of the final selection round.
